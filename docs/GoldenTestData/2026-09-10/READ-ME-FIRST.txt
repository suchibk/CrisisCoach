CRISIS COACH — PROTOTYPE
Week 5 group project · packaged 10 September 2026

RUN IT
  Mac      double-click  start.command
  Linux    ./start.sh
  Windows  open a terminal here and run:  python -m http.server 8000

THEN OPEN
  http://localhost:8000/crisis-coach-prototype.html

Use Chrome or Edge. Do NOT double-click the .html file itself — opening it
as file:// blocks the microphone and the demo will look broken.

VOICE
  Tap the microphone once. It keeps listening and answers out loud.
  It only works on http://localhost — not on a phone over wifi, and not
  on iPhone at all (Safari and iOS Chrome cannot do web speech input).

Full instructions and the test script are in
  docs/Crisis Coach - Deployment and Test.docx
